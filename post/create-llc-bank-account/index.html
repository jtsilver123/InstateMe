6. Create LLC Bank Account

By Jake Silver·Oct 25, 2022·2 min read·Updated Aug 20, 2025

Congratulations on becoming a new business owner! As an entrepreneur, it is important to separate your personal finances from those of your LLC by opening a separate business bank account. This will not only help organize your finances but also demonstrate the legitimacy of your business to the residency's office.

Please Note: The instructions provided below will guide you through the process of applying for a business bank account. If the bank requests proof of address, you can submit a copy of your current lease.

  • You may collect payments via personal Venmo (or a different app) before setting up the bank account, but ensure that you deposit the business funds into the bank account once it's established.

Please Note: If you haven't already, you need to submit the BOIR Report. It takes <5 minutes and will ensure your LLC is federally compliant.
