How Aggies get there

The Texas domicile pathway

Live in Texas 12 months

Establish a fixed, permanent home in Texas for the 12 consecutive months before reclassification.

Show intent to stay

Gainful employment, residential property, or operating a business in Texas — plus a Texas driver’s license, vehicle registration, and voter registration.

Petition your university

Submit the residency questionnaire with complete documentation before A&M’s census-date deadline. We handle the details with you.
