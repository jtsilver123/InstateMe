Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our number one priority.

When signing up with InstateMe you can be sure that we pay the utmost attention to keeping your data safe and private.

Privacy Statement

InstateMe (“we,” “us,” and “our”) seeks to safeguard your privacy and secure your information during your visit to our web site and your use of our web site services. This privacy policy outlines our practices for collection, use and disclosure of the information that you provide to us through this web site. By visiting this web site or using our services, you consent to the procedures and practices discussed in this policy. We may, from time to time, change this privacy policy, so please check it regularly for any changes or updates. If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, then you may email us at jsilver@instateme.com.

SMS Communication

If you opt in to receive SMS messages from InstateMe, we may collect and use your phone number to send transactional, account-related text messages about your InstateMe account and residency checklist tasks. These messages may include task reminders, overdue task notices, checklist updates, and account notifications.

Message frequency varies based on your account activity and task deadlines, typically 1-5 messages per week. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. You can request help by replying HELP or by contacting us at jsilver@instateme.com.

SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase or use of InstateMe.

No mobile information, including phone numbers or SMS opt-in consent, will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Key Terms

Throughout this privacy policy, we use several specialized terms. When we use the term “Personally Identifiable Information” in this privacy policy, we mean information that is specific to you as individual, like your name, email address, telephone number, and other employment-related information and credit card information. The term “User Information” incorporates all of your Personally Identifiable Information and any other forms of information discussed in this privacy policy. We use the term “aggregate” to indicate our combination of information from various persons or users. Information is said to be “anonymized” if it does not identify individual persons or entities, or associate a particular person or entity with such information.
