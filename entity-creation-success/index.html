To-do’s while the state reviews your LLC

InstateMe will send your info to the state and follow up after your LLC is approved (approximately 7 days).

1

Acquire lease

Reach out to your current landlord or your university’s housing office to request a proof of address (this is typically a lease). We will use this to form a business bank account!

2

Set up Venmo

As a business owner, you’ll need a quick and easy way to collect payments from your customers. Any mobile payment app does the trick — secure, convenient, and helps you get paid faster.

3

Schedule DPS

Schedule your Texas license appointment. Odds are the appointment is months out and that’s okay! We just need you to have either a driver license or state ID card within 10 months.

After the state approves your LLC, InstateMe will create a personal dashboard for you and reach out with next steps!
