Beneficial Ownership Information Report (BOIR)

By Jake Silver·Feb 14, 2024·2 min read·Updated Aug 7, 2024

The Corporate Transparency Act (CTA), aimed at combating illicit financial activity, went into effect on January 1, 2024. Under the act, small businesses across the United States need to file the beneficial ownership information report (takes < 5 minutes).

When to report (based on filling date found within Articles of Organization folder):

  • Companies created or registered before January 1, 2024 must file their initial report by January 1, 2025

  • Companies created or registered on or after January 1, 2024 must file their initial report within 90 days of receiving notice of their creation or registration

Filling Instructions

  1. Go to https://boiefiling.fincen.gov/

  2. Click "File BOIR"

  3. Hit "File Online BOIR"

Filing Form

2. Date prepared: auto-filled

8. Tax Identification number: Employer Identification Number (EIN) is located in the top right of your EIN document (e.g., xx-xxxxxxx) -- when copy/pasting it into the system, remove the hyphen

9. Country/Juridiction: Skip

10.

a. Country/Jurisdiction of formation: United States of America

b. State of formation: Select state

11-15. Current Address: This should be your most updated business address within your residency state

Company Applicant(s)

16. Existing reporting company: Check this box if the reporting company was created or registered before January 1, 2024 (you don't need to complete this section). Do not check the box if the reporting company was created or registered on or after January 1, 2024 (you need to complete this section). You can check when your company was created by opening your Articles of Formation and seeing when your document was filled.
