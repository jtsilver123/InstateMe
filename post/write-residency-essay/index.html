17. Write Residency Essay

By Jake Silver·Dec 5, 2024·3 min read

By crafting a clear, compelling narrative that explains why you’ve chosen this particular state and how you plan to integrate into its communities, you provide evidence that your residency claim isn’t just procedural—it’s genuine. Adding this essay to your application not only supports your case by showcasing your personal motivations, career ambitions, and cultural interests, but it also helps reviewers understand the depth of your commitment. Through this guide, you’ll learn how to create an essay that tells your story, strengthens your residency claim, and offers a meaningful glimpse into your future as a fully integrated resident.


Note: The entire "essay" is typically between 4 and 12 sentences in length! Feel free to use ChatGPT for help!

  1. Start with a Blank Document

    1. Open a new document in your preferred word processor (e.g., Google Docs or Microsoft Word). Having a clean slate will help you focus on organizing your thoughts clearly.

  2. Reflect on Your Motivations

    Take a moment to think deeply about why you want to establish residency in Texas. Avoid educational factors...so think about reasons such as:

    1. Jot down the points that resonate most with your personal situation. Feel free to think of other motivations not listed here.

    1. Family Ties: Being closer to immediate family members who live in Texas and benefiting from their support network.

    2. Job Opportunities: Taking advantage of Texas’ thriving industries—like energy, technology, healthcare, or aerospace—to enhance your career path.

    3. Affordable Cost of Living: Stretching your budget further in a state known for reasonable housing and living expenses.

    4. Cultural Diversity and Experiences: Embracing the rich cultural landscape, traditions, events, and cuisines found throughout the state.

    5. Networking and Professional Connections: Building relationships with local professionals, mentors, and organizations to boost career prospects.

    6. Natural Beauty and Outdoor Activities: Enjoying Texas’ beaches, national parks, and scenic areas to enrich your lifestyle outside of school or work.

  3. Craft a Strong Opening Statement

    1. Begin writing your essay with a clear, direct, and impactful opening. Briefly introduce your desire to become a Texas resident and set a positive, genuine tone for the rest of the essay.

  4. Highlight Your Primary Reason for Moving to Texas (Non-Educational)

    1. Focus on a central motivation that is unrelated to your education. Whether it’s joining a family business, accessing local job markets, or embracing the cultural environment, this reason should stand out as a key driver for your decision.

  5. Detail a Timeline of Your Move

    1. Provide a chronological outline of when and how you arrived in Texas. Emphasize the non-educational steps you’ve taken since moving—such as finding housing, starting a job, joining community groups, or attending local events—to show your commitment to integrating into Texas life.

  6. Explain Your Immediate Plans as a Texas Resident

    1. Share what you aim to accomplish in the near future. For example, are you looking forward to professional networking opportunities, participating in community activities, or exploring cultural festivals and traditions? This shows active engagement rather than a passive presence.

  7. Outline Your Long-Term Plans in Texas

    1. Discuss how you envision thriving in Texas over the long haul—beyond the timeframe of any educational programs. Highlight how you see yourself growing a career, contributing to the community, and enjoying the lifestyle that Texas provides.

  8. Connect Texas to Your Future

    1. Illustrate how Texas aligns with your personal and professional aspirations. Maybe the state’s economy supports your entrepreneurial ambitions, or its cultural richness complements your personal interests and desired way of life.

  9. Express Your Commitment to the Texas Community

    1. Show that you’re not just living in Texas, but actively invested in becoming part of its fabric. Mention ways you plan to give back, whether through community service, supporting local businesses, joining professional associations, or participating in civic life.

  10. Declare Your Sole Residency Intent

    1. Clearly state that you intend to make Texas your sole state of residency. Confirm that you will relinquish residency ties with any other state and fully establish your life in Texas.

  11. Review for Clarity and Accuracy

    1. Read through your entire essay. Ensure it’s easy to understand, free of errors, and focuses on personal, non-educational motivations. Make sure each section logically supports your decision to reside in Texas and reflects genuine, well-considered reasoning.

  12. Download and Save Your Document

    1. Once satisfied with your essay, save and download it. Keep it stored securely for future reference or submission.
