7. Create a Logo

By Jake Silver·Dec 7, 2023·1 min read·Updated Jan 3, 2025

Time to get creative! Designing a distinctive logo through AI not only streamlines the branding process but also underscores the professionalism of your business to potential customers

Please Note: Feel free to employ any digital software of your choice for logo design, in addition to the recommendation provided below. The specific software you opt for is entirely up to you and will not impact the outcome.

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Navigate to logo.com

  2. Type your company name

  3. Click "Make a Logo"

  4. Type your company slogan if applicable

  5. Click "Next"

  6. Select which describes your business

  7. Type your industry

  8. Click "Next"

  9. Enter your keywords

  10. Click "Next"

  11. Hover over a logo you like

  12. Click "Continue"

  13. Click "Get Logo"

  14. Click "Create this Brand"

  15. Check "Do not send me emails"

  16. Create an account

  17. Click "Get Started" under "Free"

  18. Click "I am not sure yet"

  19. Click the close symbol

  20. Click "Download"

  21. Click "Download"

  22. Upload a PDF of your logo into your InstateMe portal

  23. Update Checklist "Status" column accordingly
