8. Create a Website

By Jake Silver·Dec 7, 2023·1 min read·Updated Nov 19, 2024

Having a website is essential for businesses today. It establishes credibility, expands reach, and serves as a 24/7 marketing tool. With a professional online presence, you can engage customers, showcase products, and build lasting relationships that drive success.

Please Note: I want some web presence. If you have a different type of site (like Poshmark, Amazon, etc) that is okay and you do not need to complete the steps below.

Please Note: Feel free to employ any digital software of your choice for website design, in addition to the recommendation provided below. The specific software you opt for is entirely up to you and will not impact the outcome.

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Navigate to Wix

    1. This should be free. No need to have custom domain.

  2. Click “Create with AI”

  3. Create an account

  4. Click “Start Chat”

  5. Type what your business is

  6. Type your business name

  7. Type what your business sells

  8. Type what you want on your website

  9. Type your connection to the business topic

  10. Click “Generate Site”

  11. Click either option to further personalize the site

  12. Click “Continue with this Design”

  13. Click “Continue to the Editor”

  14. Click anywhere on your website to hide the sidebar

  15. Click “Publish”

  16. Click “View Site”

  17. Copy the URL

  18. Take a screenshot of the website

  19. Upload to folder
