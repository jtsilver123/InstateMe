12. Voter Registration

By Jake Silver·Dec 7, 2023·2 min read·Updated Aug 16, 2025

As mentioned previously, establishing intent to make Texas your primary residence is a crucial aspect of the residency process. Registering to vote in the state (you did this at the DMV) is a key step in demonstrating your commitment to becoming a Texas resident and will strengthen your position in the process.

Please Note: If you have not received your registration card within 30 days of your DMV appointment, you may follow the steps below to reapply

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Navigate to the voter registration page

  2. Click "Start a voter registration application here"

  3. Select "Yes" for "Are you a United States Citizen"

  4. Select "Yes" for "Will you be 18 years of age on or before election day?"

  5. Select "New" for "Application Type"

  6. Select "No" for "Are you interested in serving as an election worker"

  7. Type your first name

  8. Type your middle name if applicable

  9. Type your last name

  10. Enter your date of birth

  11. Enter the last 4 digits of your SSN

  12. Select your county

  13. Type your address with apartment number

  14. Type your city

  15. Type your zip code

  16. Type your mailing address info if mail cannot be delivered to your residence address

  17. Click "Continue"

  18. Verify your information is correct

  19. Click "Print Application"

  20. Print the application

  21. Sign the form to certify that all information is accurate

  22. Enter today's date

  23. Mail the completed application directly to your country's election office
