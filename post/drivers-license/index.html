Getting Your Texas Driver's License

By Jake Silver·Jan 6, 2023·2 min read·Updated Jan 29, 2024

This step-by-step guide is written for InstateMe clients working through their personalized residency checklist — but it’s free for everyone. Questions? Email us or book a free call.

Establishing intent to make Texas your primary residence is a crucial aspect of the residency process. Obtaining a Texas driver’s license or state ID card, and registering to vote in the state (you do this at the DMV), are key steps in demonstrating your commitment to becoming a Texas resident and will strengthen your position in the process.

Please note: when at the DMV, check the box that asks if you would like to vote in Texas. Once you do this, they will send you a card within 30 days.

Please note: if you have a car (or are planning to have one) in Texas, please register it in Texas.

Before your appointment

  1. For any specific queries, reach out to the DPS Driver License support team at (512) 424-2600 or via the Texas DPS website.
  2. Gather the required documents — read this section carefully:
    • Review the general requirements on the DPS site.
    • If you lack “Texas Vehicle Registration and Proof of Insurance,” that’s okay — you will mark that on your application. To acquire it, use our vehicle registration guide.
    • Use the DPS REAL ID Document Check Application to get a customized checklist of documents to bring. (If the link isn’t working, Google “How to Apply for a Texas Driver License” and click “REAL ID Document Check Application.”)
    • Only legitimate originals are allowed — photocopies will not be accepted.
    • All documents proving Texas residency must contain your name and Texas residential address (including apartment number), and must be printed.
    • Struggling to find proof of Texas residency? Register to vote first — the voter certificate is acceptable evidence.
    • Bank letters are not accepted — only printed bank statements.

At your appointment

  1. Arrive 30 minutes early to fill out the application.
  2. Click “Yes” when asked, “If you are a U.S. citizen, would you like to register to vote?” (If you’re already registered you can decline.)
  3. Provide your documentation to the DPS agent.
  4. Provide your signature and thumbprints, and have your picture taken.
  5. Pay the $33 application fee and pass the vision exam.
  6. Provide a Texas address for your license to be shipped to.

Afterwards

  • Upload photos of your license (or ID card) into your InstateMe portal.
  • Update the “Status” column within your checklist.
