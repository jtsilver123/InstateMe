FAQs

Let’s make this even easier

What is InstateMe and how can it help me?

InstateMe specializes in guiding students to obtain in-state tuition rates by helping them establish residency in their chosen state. We offer expert support throughout the process, ensuring compliance with state-specific requirements.

What does InstateMe cost?

InstateMe charges an initial refundable fee, which covers the comprehensive services we provide to ensure your successful establishment of in-state residency. Upon achieving this status, our fee is 100% of your first semester of savings. This typically represents about 10–20% of your total education savings.

Note that this fee is only applied to your initial semester savings, not the entire duration of your education. Beyond the first semester, you keep every dollar of continued savings with no additional fees from us. Our pricing is transparent and structured to align with the financial benefits we deliver.

Can InstateMe guarantee that I will obtain in-state tuition?

No — and you should be skeptical of anyone who says otherwise. What we offer instead is a risk-free structure: if you do not qualify for in-state tuition after following our guidance, you do not pay us. So far, 100% of our clients have achieved in-state tuition status.

How long does it typically take to establish residency?

Most states require a physical presence for at least 12 months. However, the entire process can vary depending on individual circumstances and state requirements.
