Beneficial Ownership Information Report (BOIR)

By Jake Silver·Feb 14, 2024·2 min read·Updated Aug 6, 2024

This step-by-step guide is written for InstateMe clients working through their personalized residency checklist — but it’s free for everyone. Questions? Email us or book a free call.

The Corporate Transparency Act (CTA), aimed at combating illicit financial activity, went into effect on January 1, 2024. Under the act, small businesses across the United States need to file the Beneficial Ownership Information Report (takes under 5 minutes).

When to report (based on the filing date found within your Articles of Organization folder):

  • Companies created or registered before January 1, 2024 must file their initial report by January 1, 2025.
  • Companies created or registered on or after January 1, 2024 must file within 90 days of receiving notice of their creation or registration.

Filing instructions

  1. Go to boiefiling.fincen.gov
  2. Click “File BOIR” → “File Online BOIR”

Filing form

  • 1. Type of filing: Initial Report
  • 2. Date prepared: auto-filled
  • 3. Request to receive FinCEN ID: check
  • 4. Foreign pooled investment vehicle: skip
  • 5. Reporting company legal name: copy/paste the legal name from your Articles of Organization
  • 6. Alternative name: skip
  • 7. Tax identification type: EIN
  • 8. Tax identification number: your EIN is in the top right of your EIN document (e.g., xx-xxxxxxx) — remove the hyphen when pasting
  • 9. Country/Jurisdiction: skip
  • 10a. Country of formation: United States of America · 10b. State of formation: select your state
  • 11–15. Current address: your most updated business address within your residency state

Company applicant(s)

  • 16. Existing reporting company: check this box if the company was created before January 1, 2024 (then skip this section). Don’t check it if created on or after January 1, 2024 (then complete this section). Check your Articles of Formation for the filing date.
  • 17–18: skip
  • 19–23: basic info about yourself
  • 24–29: your residential address
  • 30–33: upload a form of identification

Beneficial owner(s)

  • 35–37: skip
  • 38–42: basic info about yourself
  • 43–47: your residential address (use your new residency-state address)
  • 48–51: upload identification (it’s okay if the documentation shows a different address)

Submit

  • Submit the final application, download the transcript, and upload the file into your Certificate of Formation folder.
