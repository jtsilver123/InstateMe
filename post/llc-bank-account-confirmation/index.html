10. LLC Bank Account Confirmation

By Jake Silver·Dec 7, 2023·1 min read·Updated Dec 12, 2023

With your successfully created bank account, you're now set to effectively organize your business finances.

Please Note: If you are having trouble setting up your account, please email our team

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Navigate to your Novo account and Log in

  2. Select “Account Info” on the left sidebar

  3. Hit “Bank Letter” in the upper right to download your confirmation letter

  4. Click “Download Bank Letter”

  5. Upload the PDF of your Bank Letter into your InstateMe portal
