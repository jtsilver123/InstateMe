State-by-state rules

Residency rules, decoded

Every state writes its own rules for in-state tuition — and every university interprets them differently. Start with our state guides below, then talk to us about your specific school.

TX

Texas Residency Rules

To qualify for Texas in-state tuition, establish domicile by living in Texas 12 months and showing intent through work, property, or a business.

Read the Texas guide →
UT

Utah Residency Rules

To qualify for Utah in-state tuition, live in Utah for 12 continuous months, prove physical presence, and show intent to stay.

Read the Utah guide →
AR

Arkansas Residency Rules

To qualify for Arkansas in-state tuition, establish domicile by living there six months and showing intent through work, property, or state taxes.

Read the Arkansas guide →
